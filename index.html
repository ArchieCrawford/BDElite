THE PROGRAM

A 7v7 football program built to develop elite athletes on and off the field

BD Elite is a new 7v7 football program launching in 2027. We're building something different: a program where young athletes get real coaching, real competition, and a real brotherhood — not just a jersey.

With teams at 8U, 10U, and 18U, every player gets developed at their level and pushed to the next one. Tryout dates are coming soon — sign up below so you're the first to know.

THE 2027 SEASON

What BD Elite families can expect — straight from our welcome letter

🎒

What You Get

Every athlete receives two uniforms and a BD Elite backpack — proper team gear to represent BD Elite with pride at practices and events.

Free Team Media by Media Kingz.

Individual package available for an extra cost — $40 deposit.

💵

No Registration Fee. No Team Fee.

Instead of a traditional fee, we keep it simple with an event-based contribution:

$30 per player, per event

This helps cover the costs of each event your athlete participates in.

🏟️

10+ Events

The 2027 schedule is expected to include ten or more events — including three out-of-state tournaments. Locations, dates, travel expectations, and any tournament costs will be shared as plans are finalized.

🗣️

Communication & Transparency

We'll give families timely info on practices, events, schedules, expectations, travel, and costs. In return, we ask you to communicate openly with the coaching staff about attendance, conflicts, questions, and concerns — so everyone knows what's expected before committing.
